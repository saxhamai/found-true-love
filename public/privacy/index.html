Privacy

Found True Love is designed to work entirely on your device. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or any contact or relationship details.

Your quiz answers and language preference are stored only in your browser's local storage, purely so your progress isn't lost on an accidental refresh. They are never sent to a server, and you can clear them at any time by retaking the test.

This is a placeholder privacy page for the MVP and will be expanded as the product grows.
